# Nous Browser 1.4.6

## 中文

- **固定分组的展开／收起按窗口分别记住**：在一个窗口里收起固定分组，不再让其他窗口里的同一分组也跟着收起。分组的名称、图标和其中的标签仍在所有窗口间同步；每个窗口重新打开时恢复自己的展开状态。

## English

- **Pinned groups open and close per window**: collapsing a pinned group in one window no longer collapses it in your other windows. Its name, icon and tabs still stay in sync everywhere, and each window remembers its own open and closed groups.
