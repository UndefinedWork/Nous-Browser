# Nous Browser 1.4.11

## 中文

- **侧边栏空间切换重写**：每个空间的位置只由当前空间决定，在任何空间里展开或收起分组都不会再让侧边栏错位、闪动，或露出其他空间的标签。双指左右轻扫仍可切换空间，到达第一个或最后一个空间时会有回弹。
- **分组展开收起更干净**：收起时离开的标签在分组内部快速淡出，不再短暂叠在下方的分组上。

## English

- **Rewritten space paging in the sidebar**: each space's position comes only from the current space, so expanding or collapsing a group in any space no longer shifts the sidebar, flickers or reveals tabs from another space. Two-finger horizontal swipes still switch spaces, with a bounce at the first and last space.
- **Cleaner group expand and collapse**: tabs leaving a closing group fade quickly inside it instead of briefly overlapping the group below.
