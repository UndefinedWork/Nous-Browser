# Nous Browser 1.4.17

## 中文

- **导航按钮图标更统一**：侧边栏底部后退、前进和刷新按钮的图标缩小为 12 点，与侧边栏其他按钮（展开/收起、新建、资料库）的图标大小一致；按钮本身和可点击区域大小不变。

## English

- **Consistent navigation icons**: the back, forward and reload icons at the bottom of the sidebar are now 12 points, the same size as the other sidebar button icons (sidebar toggle, Add New, Library). The buttons and their click areas keep their size.
