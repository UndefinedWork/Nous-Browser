# Nous Browser 1.4.15

## 中文

- **侧边栏使用系统原生的窗口按钮**：开启“在侧边栏显示窗口按钮”后，关闭、最小化和全屏按钮现在是 macOS 自己的红黄绿按钮，而不是仿制的图形：外观、窗口失焦时的灰色、悬停时显示的 ×、− 和全屏符号、按钮行为（绿色按钮遵循系统的全屏/缩放设置）以及辅助功能都与系统一致。它们跟随侧边栏布局，在收起的侧边栏中按比例缩小，进入全屏时隐藏。

## English

- **Native window buttons in the sidebar**: with “Show window buttons in the sidebar” on, close, minimize and full screen are now macOS’s own traffic lights instead of drawn look-alikes. Their look, the grey of an inactive window, the ×, − and full-screen symbols on hover, their actions (green follows the system’s full-screen/zoom setting) and accessibility all match the system. They follow the sidebar’s layout, scale down in the collapsed sidebar and step aside in full screen.
