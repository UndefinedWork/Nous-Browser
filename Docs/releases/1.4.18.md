# Nous Browser 1.4.18

## 中文

- **退出全屏后标签列表不再消失**：在 macOS 27 上，网页进入全屏（例如视频或摄像头全屏）后按 Esc 退出，侧边栏的标签列表会变成空白。现在标签列表不受窗口标题栏留白的影响，退出网页或窗口全屏后都会重新显示。
- **左右轻扫切换空间带回弹**：松开手指后，列表会以轻微回弹的动画停到位置上；即使触控板没有发出手势结束的信号，列表也会在停止移动后自动归位，不会停在两个空间之间。

## English

- **The tab list no longer disappears after full screen**: on macOS 27, leaving a page's full screen (a video or camera view, for example) with Esc left the sidebar's tab list blank. The list now ignores the window's title-bar insets and shows again after leaving page or window full screen.
- **Space swipes spring into place**: when you let go, the list settles with a slight bounce. If the trackpad never reports the end of a gesture, the list settles by itself once it stops moving, instead of staying between two spaces.
