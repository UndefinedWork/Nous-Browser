# Nous Browser 1.4.13

## 中文

- **“新建”菜单**：侧边栏顶部的 + 按钮现在打开一个菜单，集中了新建标签页、新建分组、新建窗口、空间（切换到任一空间或新建空间）、截图、录屏和设置，每一项都带图标，截图和录屏显示你在设置里选择的快捷键。
- **标签列表末尾的“新建标签页”**：列表最后一行是一个安静的 + 新建标签页入口，悬停时浮起；收起的侧边栏里显示为一个 +。
- **空间圆点最多显示 4 个，并跟手**：空间再多，底部也只显示 4 个圆点，边缘缩小的圆点提示两侧还有更多空间，侧边栏收窄时不再溢出。轻扫时圆点随手指连续移动，当前胶囊在两个空间之间拉伸过渡，不再在切换结束时跳变或闪出残影。悬停圆点时会加深并放大。
- **去掉切换后的空间名提示**：展开的侧边栏里，圆点已经实时显示位置，切换完成后不再弹出空间名浮层（收起的侧边栏仍保留小指示）。
- **更精致的顶部按钮**：侧边栏顶部的两个按钮缩小为 32×32，图标也相应变小；展开和收起的侧边栏都按新设计稿对齐。
- **轻扫切换空间更可靠**：某些输入工具转发的触控板滚动不带手势阶段，以前这类轻扫会被忽略，现在同样能切换空间。
- **窗口按钮对齐**：红黄绿三个窗口按钮与侧边栏顶部按钮垂直居中对齐。
- **资料库重新整理**：标题、搜索和 iCloud 状态在一行，分区标签单独一行，窄窗口下可横向滚动，不再挤压或错位；去掉了截图和录屏按钮（用快捷键或“新建”菜单）。分区按使用频率排序：全部、历史、书签、下载、截图、录屏、更新。“全部”页以历史记录为主，宽窗口下书签和下载并排在右侧；下载只在进行中或 24 小时内才出现；截图只显示一行。
- **更流畅**：滚动列表、展开收起侧边栏或分组、切换空间时，侧边栏不再每一帧重新渲染全部内容，并且只构建当前空间和相邻空间的列表，明显减少掉帧。

## English

- **“Add New” menu**: the + button at the top of the sidebar now opens a menu with New Tab, New Group, New Window, Spaces (switch to any space or create one), Screenshot, Record Screen and Settings, each with an icon. Screenshot and Record Screen show the shortcuts chosen in Settings.
- **“New Tab” at the end of the tab list**: the list ends with a quiet + New Tab row that lifts on hover; the collapsed sidebar shows it as a +.
- **At most four space dots, following your fingers**: the bottom bar shows four dots, with smaller edge dots hinting at more spaces beyond, so a narrow sidebar no longer overflows. During a swipe the dots move continuously and the current capsule stretches between spaces, instead of jumping or leaving an afterimage at the end. Dots darken and grow on hover.
- **No space-name banner after switching**: in the wide sidebar the dots already show where you are, so the floating name no longer appears once the pages settle (the collapsed sidebar keeps its small indicator).
- **Refined top buttons**: the two buttons at the top of the sidebar are now 32×32 with smaller icons, and both the wide and the collapsed sidebar follow the new design.
- **More reliable space swipes**: trackpad scrolling re-posted by some input tools carries no gesture phases; those swipes used to be ignored and now switch spaces too.
- **Window buttons aligned**: the close, minimize and zoom buttons are vertically centred with the sidebar's top buttons.
- **Reorganised Library**: the title, search and iCloud status share one row and the sections get their own row that scrolls sideways in a narrow window, so nothing squeezes or overlaps. The screenshot and record buttons are gone (use the shortcuts or the Add New menu). Sections are ordered by how often they are used: All, History, Bookmarks, Downloads, Screenshots, Recordings, Updates. The All page leads with History, with Bookmarks and Downloads beside it in a wide window; Downloads only appear while active or from the last 24 hours; Captures take a single row.
- **Smoother**: scrolling the list, collapsing the sidebar or a group and switching spaces no longer re-render the whole sidebar on every frame, and only the current and neighbouring spaces' lists are built, cutting dropped frames.
