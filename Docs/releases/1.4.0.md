# Nous Browser 1.4.0

## 中文

**内核换为 Chromium（NousCore），并加入空间、分组、资料库、截图与录屏。**

- **空间**：每个空间有独立的固定项、分组和标签页；在侧边栏左右滑动或点击底部圆点切换。
- **固定与分组**：固定的网页在关闭窗口后仍保留；右键新建分组，可拖入拖出、改名、换图标、整体固定。
- **三合一导航**：后退、前进、刷新合并为一个玻璃控件。
- **资料库（⇧⌘L）**：书签、截图、录屏、历史、下载进度和应用更新集中查看，默认通过 iCloud 云盘同步。
- **书签（⌘D）**：在资料库管理，地址推荐和历史中带星标。
- **截图（⌥⇧⌘S）与录屏（⌥⇧⌘R）**：完成后在新标签打开编辑器，支持背景、圆角、阴影、箭头等标注，以及跟随点击缩放、光标平滑的录屏导出。录制时非录制区域会变暗。快捷键可在设置中修改，且在任何应用中可用。
- 网页滚动条悬停时只加粗，不再显示轨道。

**升级须知**

- 从 1.1.x 升级时内核由 WebKit 换为 Chromium，**需要重新登录网站一次**。标签、窗口、历史和设置继续保留。
- 从此版本起，后续更新会**保留网站登录和保存的密码**，不会弹出钥匙串密码框。
- Apple Silicon，macOS 26 及以上。当前为 ad-hoc 签名，尚未经过 Developer ID 签名与 Apple 公证。

## English

**Chromium (NousCore) engine, plus spaces, groups, a Library, screenshots and screen recording.**

- **Spaces** with their own pins, groups and tabs; swipe the sidebar or click the dots to switch.
- **Pins** survive closing every window. **Groups** from the context menu: drag tabs in and out, rename, change the icon, pin the whole group.
- **Back, forward and reload** in one glass control.
- **Library (⇧⌘L)**: bookmarks, screenshots, recordings, history, download progress and app updates, synced through iCloud Drive by default.
- **Bookmarks (⌘D)**, starred in address suggestions and history.
- **Screenshot (⌥⇧⌘S) and screen recording (⌥⇧⌘R)** open in an editor tab: framing and annotations for images; click-following zoom, smoothed cursor and export for video. Everything outside the recorded area is dimmed while recording. Shortcuts are customizable in Settings and work in any app.
- Page scrollbars only thicken on hover; no track is drawn.

**Before you update**

- Coming from 1.1.x the engine changes from WebKit to Chromium: **sign in to websites once more**. Tabs, windows, history and settings carry over.
- From this version on, updates **keep website sign-ins and saved passwords**, without keychain password prompts.
- Apple Silicon, macOS 26 or later. Ad-hoc signed; not yet Developer ID signed or notarized.
