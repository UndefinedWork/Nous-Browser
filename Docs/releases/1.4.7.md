# Nous Browser 1.4.7

## 中文

- **画中画**：网页视频（包括视频右键菜单中的「画中画」和网站自带的画中画按钮）可以弹出为独立的悬浮播放器。圆角窗口，液态玻璃控件：关闭、返回网页、后退／前进 10 秒、播放／暂停，以及可拖动的进度条。指针移到视频上或点按视频时显示控件，播放时自动隐藏。
- **全新设置窗口**：与 Safari 一致的工具栏标签（系统液态玻璃选中效果），窗口标题随面板切换，并自动适应每个面板的高度；标签右对齐、控件居中排列，说明文字位于对应选项下方；列表采用系统样式的边框列表。

## English

- **Picture in Picture**: videos on webpages (from a video's context menu or a site's own button) open in a floating player with rounded corners and Liquid Glass controls: close, back to the page, skip back or forward 10 seconds, play/pause and a draggable progress bar. The controls appear when you point at or click the video and fade away while it plays.
- **Redesigned Settings**: Safari-style toolbar tabs with the system's Liquid Glass selection, a window titled after each pane that resizes to fit it, right-aligned labels beside a centred column of controls, notes under the settings they explain, and system-style list boxes.
