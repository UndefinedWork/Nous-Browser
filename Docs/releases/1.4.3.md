# Nous Browser 1.4.3

## 中文

- **分组拖拽不再卡住**：把标签拖进或拖出分组（包括固定区的分组）、在固定区与标签列表之间拖动时，拖动中的标签会一直跟随指针；列表保持不动，用蓝色插入线、分组高亮或固定区高亮提示落点。
- 万一拖拽手势被系统中断，松开鼠标也会完成放置，不会留下悬浮的标签。
- **网页右键菜单恢复**：链接、图片、文字的右键菜单（新标签打开、复制链接、检查等）重新出现；去掉了无效的「在无痕窗口中打开」。
- **资料库排版**：标题单独一行；分类与搜索在下一行，并与内容对齐同一栏；窗口较窄时分类移到第二行，截图网格按宽度自动排列整行。
- **可选显示窗口按钮**：设置 → 外观 →「显示窗口按钮」，在侧边栏顶部显示关闭、最小化、全屏按钮（默认关闭；隐藏侧边栏时不显示）。

## English

- **Group drags no longer get stuck**: dragging a tab into or out of a group (including pinned groups), or between pins and the tab list, keeps the tab under the pointer; the list stays put and a blue insertion line, a highlighted group or the highlighted pin area shows where it will land.
- If the drag gesture is ever interrupted, releasing the mouse still completes the drop instead of leaving a floating tab.
- **Right-click menus in web pages are back** (open link in new tab, copy link, inspect, …); the non-working "Open Link in Incognito Window" is gone.
- **Library layout**: the title has its own row; the sections and search sit below it, aligned with the content column; in a narrow window the sections move to a second row, and the captures grid fills whole rows at any width.
- **Optional window buttons**: Settings → Appearance → "Show window buttons" puts close, minimize and full-screen at the top of the sidebar (off by default; hidden while the sidebar is hidden).
