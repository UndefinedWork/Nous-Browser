# Nous Browser 1.4.2

## 中文

- **截图与录屏编辑器可缩放**：触控板双指捏合、⌘+／⌘−／⌘0，或左下角的缩放控件；放大后双指滑动平移画布。
- **通知只显示一个应用**：系统「通知」设置里不再出现名为 Chromium 的第二个应用。
- **使用 Google 登录**（如 Pinterest）恢复正常：改为在登录弹窗中完成。
- 展开的分组中拖入或拖出标签不再卡住，并显示插入位置。
- 固定 5 个及以上标签时，第一行始终铺满侧边栏。
- 右键菜单在新版 macOS 上也显示图标。
- 分组右键菜单「固定分组／取消固定分组」文字修正。
- **修复「退出」无反应**：同时打开两个以上窗口且有固定标签时，退出会被静默取消；页面无响应时也不会再卡住退出。

## English

- **Zoom in the screenshot and recording editors**: pinch, ⌘+ / ⌘− / ⌘0, or the zoom control at the bottom left; scroll with two fingers to pan.
- **One app in Notifications settings**: the second entry named "Chromium" is gone.
- **Sign in with Google** (e.g. on Pinterest) works again, in its sign-in popup.
- Dragging a tab into or out of an open group no longer gets stuck, and shows where it will land.
- With five or more pins, the first row always spans the sidebar.
- Context menus show icons on newer macOS versions too.
- Fixed the "Pin Group / Unpin Group" menu titles.
- **Quit works again** with two or more windows and pinned tabs (it was silently cancelled), and a page that never answers no longer blocks it.
