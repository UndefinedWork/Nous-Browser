# Nous Browser 1.4.16

## 中文

- **网站可以使用麦克风和摄像头了**：之前浏览器引擎会直接拒绝网站的麦克风和摄像头请求，网站因此误报“你的 Mac 阻止了麦克风”。现在网站请求时，Nous 会弹出权限提示，你的选择会按网站记住，macOS 的隐私设置照常生效。语音和视频通话等网站现在可以正常使用。
- **资料库新增“洞察”**：像“屏幕使用时间”一样查看你的浏览习惯，可按日、周、月切换，并与上一时段比较：
  - 浏览时间与每日平均，以及按小时或按天的柱状图；
  - 访问页面、搜索、打开标签页和浏览会话的次数；
  - 常用网站、各空间所占时间，以及按星期和小时显示的浏览时段热力图；
  - 专注情况（最长专注、平均专注、网站切换频率），以及新增书签、下载、截图和录屏。
- **隐私**：洞察只保存在这台 Mac 上，不会同步；只记录网站域名，不记录网页地址或内容；仅在 Nous 位于前台且你正在使用 Mac 时计时。可随时暂停记录或清除全部洞察。

## English

- **Websites can use the microphone and camera**: the browser engine used to refuse every microphone and camera request, so sites reported that the Mac was blocking the microphone. Now Nous asks you when a site requests them, remembers your answer per site, and macOS privacy settings still apply. Voice and video calls on websites work.
- **Insights in the Library**: a Screen Time–style view of your browsing by day, week or month, compared with the period before:
  - browsing time and daily average, with a chart by hour or by day;
  - pages visited, searches, tabs opened and sessions;
  - top sites, time in each space, and a weekday-by-hour heat map of when you browse;
  - focus (longest, average, how often you switch sites), plus bookmarks, downloads, screenshots and recordings added.
- **Privacy**: Insights stay on this Mac and never sync. Only each site's domain is kept, never page addresses or content, and time counts only while Nous is in front and you are using your Mac. You can pause recording or clear Insights at any time.
